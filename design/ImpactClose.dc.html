IMPACT AND BENEFITS

Sprayed where it is needed,
not everywhere.

The same flight that finds a problem also bounds it. Once a zone has a coordinate and a severity, the input follows the zone instead of the field.

Farmers

Problems located before they spread, and spray or irrigation applied to the flagged zone instead of the whole field.

FPOs and cooperatives

One aircraft serves many members, with shared scouting reports and a season-long record of every field.

Agriculture officers

Field-level maps with dated evidence photos make advisories and scheme reporting verifiable rather than anecdotal.

Nation

Lower chemical and water use per acre, and earlier warning of drought, flood and heat damage.

ECONOMIC

Targeted intervention cuts pesticide, fertiliser and pumping spend. Capital cost is about ₹80,000 against ₹6.67 lakh for an imported multispectral drone, so an FPO can own one outright.

ENVIRONMENTAL

Chemicals reach only the zones that show damage, and irrigation is advised only where soil moisture and canopy temperature both justify it — less runoff, less groundwater drawn.

SOCIAL

Diagnostics reach farms with no connectivity, in the local language, with the photograph and the confidence attached — so advice can be questioned and checked, not just obeyed.

The drone finds where the problem is, close-up vision determines what it is, ground sensors explain why it is happening, and edge AI recommends what to check next.
