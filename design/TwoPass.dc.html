TWO-PASS AUTONOMY

The survey finds where.
The revisit decides what.

One flight maps the whole field at 20–30 m and flags coordinates. The second visits only those coordinates at 3–5 m. A 5 mm insect that was under a single pixel on pass one becomes a photograph on pass two.

PASS 1 Survey grid
25 m AGL · 75/65 overlap

Fields2Cover turns the drawn polygon into a boustrophedon mission. ArduPilot flies it, the camera triggers at every waypoint, and the Jetson segments frames in the air.

PASS 2 Flagged coordinates only
4 m AGL · reviewed before flight

The hotspot map generates a second mission. A person approves it, the aircraft descends over each flagged square, and the close-up frame becomes the evidence attached to the alert.
