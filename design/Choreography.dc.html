MOTION SPEC

One drone, the whole page

A single drone lives in a fixed viewport layer. Every section registers a target — position, scale, roll, and which of the three states it wants — and scroll progress interpolates between the two nearest targets. No section owns a drone of its own.

IMPLEMENTATION

One <Drone/> in a position:fixed layer above the page. Framer Motion useScroll → useTransform maps scroll progress onto each section's registered target.

ACCESSIBILITY

Under prefers-reduced-motion the drone snaps between section positions with no scrub and no idle loops. Nothing in the page depends on the animation to be readable.

BUDGET

Transform and opacity only — no layout-triggering properties. The drone is one SVG, roughly 4 KB, drawn once and reused; below 900 px it parks in the corner at 60% scale.
